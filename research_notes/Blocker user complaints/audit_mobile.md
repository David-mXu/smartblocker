## Method and evidence limits

- **Verified** means the source note's scoped allegation, quote structure, or official boundary was supported. It does **not** mean the underlying defect was reproduced. **Qualification/correction** identifies wording that is too broad or an obsolete evidentiary label. **Omission** adds material evidence not already in the source. **Open** preserves an unresolved allegation or missing controlled test.
- The targeted Apple retrieval comprised **45 RSS feeds, 2,250 review records, and 2,183 unique review IDs**, with no feed retrieval errors. Helpful/recent feeds overlap, so records are not unique people. This purposive corpus supports themes within the sample, not prevalence or incidence among all users.
- Store and Trustpilot reviews are firsthand allegations. A developer response establishes what the vendor said, not independent causation. Vendor Apple/Android/OEM troubleshooting is likewise not independent proof of an OS defect. Policy documents establish vendor-described behavior, not a packet-level or privacy audit.
- Dates below are review or publication/update dates where available. Pages without an exposed publication date are labeled **accessed 2026-11-08**. Store-supplied app versions and review dates can change when reviews are edited; neither proves first bug appearance.
- **29 of 30 numeric US App Store review IDs cited for BlockSite/AppBlock** were confirmed in the intended product feeds. `12413257994` belongs to the separately identified **Safari listing `6470454261`**, exactly as the source note states—not the main US iPhone listing `1515753232`.
- Direct Trustpilot retrieval returned 403. Extracted Trustpilot allegations and replies therefore remain unadjudicated accounts without review-specific artifacts or receipts. Some Play retrievals encountered rate limits; only successfully captured pages are used. Absence of a retrieved review is not evidence of absence.
- No controlled reproduction was performed here for current BlockSite 8.0.1 memory or network behavior, one sec Android 3.0.0 reliability, residual Screen Time lockouts, or any alleged post-cancellation charge.

4. **Omission—recent failure and residual-block recovery.** [Review `14743481323`, 2026-11-02, iOS app 4.22](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1497465230/sortby=mostrecent/json) alleges repeated failed unlock attempts, blank screen-time pages, freezing, and session/schedule conflict. Separately, Opal's [hard-reset guide](https://help.opalapp.com/article/how-do-i-hard-reset-my-screen-time-settings) (accessed 2026-11-08) explicitly addresses blocks surviving account/app deletion or permission revocation: reinstall, run a five-minute Session, test, and contact human support if restrictions remain. 5. **Open—new billing allegation, not a verified charge or recurrence rate.** [Review `14733995024`, 2026-10-31, 4.21.1](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1497465230/sortby=mostrecent/json) alleges an **$80** charge after cancellation and deletion but provides no receipt, subscription record, cancellation timestamp, or store-account evidence. ## Cross-product paid-review / SEO integrity conclusion

**No product-specific campaign record, payment/procurement evidence, platform enforcement action, or regulatory finding establishing purchased reviews or coordinated rating manipulation was found for Opal, one sec, ScreenZen, Forest, BlockSite, or AppBlock in the collected evidence.** Repeated wording, clusters of favorable reviews, generic five-star praise, duplicate reviewer posts, negative-store allegations, and highly ranked “alternative/best blocker” posts may justify scrutiny; they do not prove payment or coordination.

Competitor/commercial SEO material—such as FOCO writing about ScreenZen, Blok about one sec, FaithLock about ScreenZen, or ZalipOff about BlockSite—has competitive/affiliate incentives and should be treated only as a lead, not independent corroboration of product allegations. Conversely, the absence of substantiating evidence does not prove that no incentivized review or undisclosed marketing ever occurred; it means **no paid-review or manipulation conclusion should be added to these notes on the present record**.
