# Newer mobile blocker complaints: Roots, Jomo, Refocus, and BePresent

**Scope and evidence rules.** Research cutoff: **2026-11-08**. This note covers only Roots: Screen Time Control (`id6446800962`), Jomo - Screen Time Blocker (`id1609960918`), Refocus: App Blocker & Lock (`id1645639057`), and BePresent: Screen Time Control (`id1644737181`, Android `com.bepresent.android`). Store metadata, official pages, and comparison pages were accessed on the cutoff date unless a page supplies its own publication/update date. “Recurring” means at least two independent posts about a similar theme in the sampled source; it does **not** mean common among all users. “Isolated” means one located report, not proof that only one user experienced it. Store reviews are firsthand allegations, not independently reproduced defects; review dates and store-supplied app versions do not necessarily identify when a defect began.

**Review-sample calibration.** The US Apple most-recent RSS snapshot produced **295** Roots reviews over six non-empty pages (47 one-star, 18 two-star), **341** Jomo reviews over seven (28, 19), and the first **500** each for Refocus (21, 3) and BePresent (60, 11), capped at ten pages. Refocus and BePresent therefore have truncated samples, while none of these counts is a whole-store distribution or complaint-incidence estimate. — [Roots page 1](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=6446800962/sortby=mostrecent/json), [terminal Roots page 6](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=6/id=6446800962/sortby=mostrecent/json); [Jomo page 1](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1609960918/sortby=mostrecent/json), [terminal Jomo page](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=7/id=1609960918/sortby=mostrecent/json); [Refocus page 1](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1645639057/sortby=mostrecent/json), [page 10 cutoff](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=10/id=1645639057/sortby=mostrecent/json); [BePresent page 1](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1644737181/sortby=mostrecent/json), [page 10 cutoff](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=10/id=1644737181/sortby=mostrecent/json).

#### Privacy scope and sensitive permissions

- **Roots:** its store description says phone-usage data stays on the phone through Apple’s Screen Time API. The same App Store privacy panel nevertheless declares identifiers that may be used for tracking, and contact info, identifiers, usage data, and diagnostics that may be collected and linked to the user. This is a scope distinction worth explaining—detailed Screen Time content versus account/analytics data—not proof that Roots uploads every app-usage record or sells data. — [US App Store description and developer-declared privacy panel, accessed 2026-11-08](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/roots-screen-time-control/id6446800962).
- **Jomo:** the policy updated **2026-01-23** says it collects goals/profile information and blocking configuration and names third parties including **Amplitude, Bugsnag, Crisp, Facebook, and OpenAI**, while saying it never sells personal information. The store description also says Jomo has no access to private content. The defensible reading is that content access is restricted but account/configuration/service-provider data flows exist; “never sells” is not equivalent to “never collects or shares with service providers.” — [Jomo privacy policy, updated 2026-01-23](https://getjomo.notion.site/Privacy-Policy-7265a825c9fe4b289229cec614a169fd); [Jomo store description/privacy panel](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jomo-screen-time-blocker/id1609960918).
- **Refocus:** its policy updated **2026-07-03** permits collection of analytics/diagnostic and advertising identifiers, blocking settings, and optional location-based settings; detailed Screen Time reports are **primarily** processed on-device. It says blocked app/website selections are not used or shared for advertising and that personal information is not sold for money. These are specific qualified promises, not a blanket absence of data collection. — [Refocus privacy policy, updated 2026-07-03](https://refocusapp.co/privacy-policy).
- **BePresent/iOS and Android:** the iOS description says no personal data leaves the device, while its developer-declared privacy panel lists identifiers that may be used for tracking and contact info, identifiers, usage data, and other data that may be collected and linked to the user. On Android, Play describes Accessibility as detecting the foreground app during focus sessions, with no screen content, text, or keystrokes accessed and Accessibility-derived information processed locally rather than stored, transmitted, or shared. The linked Android policy separately discloses collection of per-app usage/open counts, selected or detected package/app names, account/subscription records, backend sync, Mixpanel analytics, and Twilio accountability-contact messaging. Play’s Data Safety panel declares collection of app activity and device/other IDs and “no data shared with third parties.” These statements can coexist under different data/category definitions; they do not justify extending “local” to **all** BePresent data or claiming a proven privacy-label violation. — [iOS description/privacy panel, accessed 2026-11-08](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bepresent-screen-time-control/id1644737181); [Google Play description/Data Safety, accessed 2026-11-08](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bepresent.android&hl=en_US&gl=US); [official linked Android privacy policy, accessed 2026-11-08](https://bepresent-android-privacy-policy.vercel.app/).

### Inferences

- Recent version dates show that all four products were maintained close to cutoff, but generic fixes and even named time-zone/notification fixes cannot demonstrate that older complaints are resolved. The relevant comparison is review **date/version** against the **2026-10-27 through 2026-11-05** current releases, not “updated recently, therefore fixed.” — [Roots](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=6446800962&country=us); [Jomo](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1609960918&country=us); [Refocus](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1645639057&country=us); [BePresent](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1644737181&country=us).
- Platform comparisons must not inherit stale “iOS-only” labels: Roots’ own page still lacks Android, Jomo/Refocus present Apple-platform offerings, and BePresent has a live Android package with an Android-specific permission/data model. — [Roots](https://www.getroots.app/pricing); [Jomo](https://jomo.so/); [Refocus](https://www.refocusapp.co/); [BePresent](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bepresent.android&hl=en_US&gl=US).

## What firsthand criticism exists, of which complaint types, and how recurrent or corroborated is it?

### Takeaway

The strongest repeated technical themes are **Roots’ inaccurate metering/premature limits**, **Jomo’s inconsistent limits and account/rule loss**, **Refocus’s wrong/residual blocks and porous ordinary unblocking**, and **BePresent’s trial/cancellation/support allegations plus several concrete wrong-app/onboarding failures**. Official troubleshooting documents corroborate that several Screen Time failure modes are known possibilities, but they neither verify every reviewer’s cause nor measure incidence. Battery-drain evidence is especially thin: one BePresent testimonial, not a cross-app pattern. — [Roots support](https://intercom.help/roots/en/articles/8940311-should-i-disable-share-across-devices); [Jomo limit support](https://help.jomo.so/en/article/how-to-fix-app-limits-that-arent-working-16ykc1c); [Refocus troubleshooting](https://refocusapp.co/help/notice-on-blocking-issues); [BePresent battery review, page 4, ID `14697590946`](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=4/id=1644737181/sortby=mostrecent/json).

### Inferences

- **Strictness must be evaluated by mode and recovery path.** Easy Refocus/BePresent normal-mode unblocking, Roots/Jomo accidental early blocking, and residual restrictions after uninstall are different problems. None of the cited evidence supports a universal claim that every strict/Beast/Monk mode is defeated. — [Refocus bypass reviews](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=2/id=1645639057/sortby=mostrecent/json); [BePresent hands-on mode distinction](https://www.pluggedin.com/blog/bepresent-screen-time-app-review/); [Jomo blocking guide](https://help.jomo.so/en/article/blocking-doesnt-seem-to-be-working-pr6ji2).

## Which comparison-blog or third-party claims are unsupported, outdated, or contradicted by primary evidence?

### Takeaway

The clearest errors are **BePresent described as completely free, iOS-only, and lacking a family option**, plus a single one-week/US$59.99 plan presented as the whole offer structure. Those descriptions are outdated at the 2026-11-08 cutoff, not necessarily fabricated when first published. A comparison site’s claimed “50%+” screen-time reduction is also unsupported by any linked study or method on its page. — [Compare Screen Time Apps](https://comparescreentimeapps.com/app/bepresent); [Plugged In, 2026-04-10](https://www.pluggedin.com/blog/bepresent-screen-time-app-review/); [current BePresent iOS offers](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bepresent-screen-time-control/id1644737181); [current Android package](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bepresent.android&hl=en_US&gl=US).

### Cited Findings

- **“Completely free” is contradicted by current paid offers.** Compare Screen Time Apps calls BePresent “completely free,” lists pricing as free, and says it is iOS-only with Android in development. The current App Store explicitly says “Free · In-App Purchases” and lists multiple annual/monthly/family offers; Google Play has the live `com.bepresent.android` package updated **2026-10-19**. The comparison claims should therefore not be repeated as current facts. — [Comparison page, accessed 2026-11-08](https://comparescreentimeapps.com/app/bepresent); [current iOS listing/offers](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bepresent-screen-time-control/id1644737181); [current Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bepresent.android&hl=en_US&gl=US).
- **The same comparison page does not substantiate its efficacy/comparative language.** It asserts “Proven results (50%+ reduction),” says users reported decreasing screen time by over 50%, and calls BePresent “Highest user rating,” without linking a study, sample, comparator set, or measurement method on that page. This is **unsupported by the evidence supplied there**, not proof that no users reduced usage or that the rating statement could never be reconstructed. — [Compare Screen Time Apps, accessed 2026-11-08](https://comparescreentimeapps.com/app/bepresent).
- **Plugged In’s April facts need an explicit date boundary.** Its **2026-04-10** review states a one-week trial then US$59.99 annually, “no family plan,” and iPhone-only availability with an Android waitlist. By November, the live store shows four named trial-length variants at US$59.99 plus other annual/monthly prices, a US$99.99 BePresent Pro Family (iCloud) offer, and a live Android listing. These are direct current contradictions to reusing the April summary unchanged; current evidence alone does not establish which statements were true on April 10. — [Plugged In review, 2026-04-10](https://www.pluggedin.com/blog/bepresent-screen-time-app-review/); [November iOS catalog](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bepresent-screen-time-control/id1644737181); [November Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bepresent.android&hl=en_US&gl=US).
- **Roots and Refocus illustrate why single-price comparison tables need storefront dates even where no specific blog error was verified.** Roots’ site lists US$59.99/year and US$9.99/month while Apple includes US$99.99 Premium/Pass and other variants; Refocus’s current Apple list contains US$7.99–US$59.99 Pro variants and advertising. A single-price statement can therefore omit real offers, but this note does not attribute that omission to an unexamined Roots or Refocus article. — [Roots official pricing](https://www.getroots.app/pricing); [Roots App Store offers](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/roots-screen-time-control/id6446800962); [Refocus App Store offers](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/refocus-app-blocker-lock/id1645639057).
- **Third-party hands-on criticism can be useful without treating its store facts as timeless.** Plugged In provides concrete April observations about editable ordinary blocks, non-hard daily limits, Beast Mode selection, and Safari/Firefox treatment. Those observations are relevant firsthand evidence, but they predate BePresent 0.18.104–0.18.110 and should be distinguished from the article’s now-outdated platform/price summary. — [Plugged In hands-on review, 2026-04-10](https://www.pluggedin.com/blog/bepresent-screen-time-app-review/); [BePresent current version metadata](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1644737181&country=us).

### Inferences

- “Completely free” and “iOS-only” are materially misleading **at cutoff**, while “50%+ proven reduction” is an evidence-quality problem rather than a directly disproven outcome. These require different corrections: replace the former with live store facts, and remove or independently source the latter. — [Comparison page](https://comparescreentimeapps.com/app/bepresent); [iOS offers](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bepresent-screen-time-control/id1644737181); [Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bepresent.android&hl=en_US&gl=US).
- Comparison tables should carry an observed-on date, region, platform/package ID, trial variant, and whether prices are website or store offers. Otherwise a historically plausible April description can silently become false after an Android launch or offer expansion. — [Plugged In, 2026-04-10](https://www.pluggedin.com/blog/bepresent-screen-time-app-review/); [current BePresent offers](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bepresent-screen-time-control/id1644737181).
- Store-first criticism provides stronger evidence of concrete failure modes than comparison-blog superlatives, but still cannot supply prevalence. The most defensible output is a date/version-specific allegation map plus official limitations, not a numerical ranking of which app is “worst.” — [Apple RSS method examples](https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1644737181/sortby=mostrecent/json); [Refocus official limitations](https://refocusapp.co/help/notice-on-blocking-issues).

### Gaps

- This was not an exhaustive census of every comparison article about all four products. The specifically verified current contradictions concern the two BePresent pages above; no unsupported Roots, Jomo, or Refocus comparison claim is invented merely to make the coverage symmetrical. — [Compared BePresent page](https://comparescreentimeapps.com/app/bepresent); [Plugged In](https://www.pluggedin.com/blog/bepresent-screen-time-app-review/).

- The comparison page supplies no underlying dataset for its efficacy or “highest rating” language, and this research did not locate independent evidence validating those exact comparative claims. That is an evidence gap, not a finding that BePresent has no effect. — [Compare Screen Time Apps](https://comparescreentimeapps.com/app/bepresent).
