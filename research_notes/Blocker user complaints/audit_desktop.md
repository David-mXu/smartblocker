# Desktop research notes: citation and evidence audit

Audit performed **2026-11-08** against `strict_desktop.md` and `cloud_desktop.md`. “Verified” below means the cited page or official record was retrieved and its wording checked; it does not turn a user allegation into independently reproduced technical fact.

## Source quality, link health, and quotation fidelity

### Takeaway

The notes are generally careful about version, recurrence, and firsthand-versus-official evidence. The principal weaknesses are mutable storefront/aggregate-review citations, broad bot-walling that prevents ordinary direct retrieval, one dead help link, and several specific Freedom/FocusMe attribution errors listed below.

### Cited Findings

- **Link census:** all **133 unique cited URLs** were checked with redirects on 2026-11-08. One returned HTTP 404: RescueTime’s [old multiple-computer article](https://help.rescuetime.com/article/46-can-i-install-rescuetime-on-more-than-one-computer). The other official/documentation links checked returned 200, sometimes after a redirect; HTTP 200 alone did not establish that every quotation was correct.
- **Bot-walled is not dead:** all Reddit and Trustpilot links returned HTTP 403 to plain curl. Representative Trustpilot permalinks nevertheless rendered through Firecrawl, including the [2026-03-26 Cold Turkey allegation](https://uk.trustpilot.com/reviews/69c52539762069ec568a2c8c) and [2025-12-04 FocusMe review with company updates](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/69326c42e2ab8b7ca471dcc3). [Super User](https://superuser.com/questions/1696158/how-can-i-bypass-cold-turkey-blocker) and [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90236881/apple-tight-lipped-on-removal-of-freedom-and-other-content-blocking-apps) also returned 403/CAPTCHA responses. Preserve the direct URLs, but do not describe them as freely retrievable in every client or as independently archived evidence.
- **Cold Turkey quotation check:** the 2026-03-26 review really does allege browser-blocking failure, applications closing after 60 seconds, BIOS/boot-keyboard interference, System Restore, and Chrome reinstallation; its headline says “nearly destroyed years of work” but its body identifies no lost documents. The [2026-07-07 factory-reset review](https://uk.trustpilot.com/reviews/6ac27edf7df5762524253cc6) likewise supports the notes’ cautious paraphrase and expressly asks others to repost its slogans. That page was **updated 2026-10-04**, while the review itself is dated July 7; use the latter as the original-post date.
- **FocusMe quotation check:** the [Chris review updated 2024-11-28](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/66fcb1f066208e3a0a172409) supports the full-refund, high-CPU, freezing, notification, overlay, and possible multi-monitor qualifications. The [Shawn review/company exchange dated 2025-12-04 through 2025-12-09](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/69326c42e2ab8b7ca471dcc3) supports Zoom-assisted removal, continued failure, Windows login trouble, and the vendor’s M4 Sequoia/Tahoe test claim—not customer confirmation of a fix.
- **Focus and RescueTime review fidelity:** the two [Focus reviews dated 2025-02-07 and 2025-03-30](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/heyfocus.com) match the notes’ statistics/support and “stopped working entirely” quotations. The [RescueTime reviews dated 2026-07-07, 2026-05-21, and updated 2023-05-20](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.rescuetime.com) match the two-seat/refusal, post-install card/privacy objection, and refunded-charge accounts. Focus has only two visible Trustpilot reviews; these are firsthand anecdotes, not a support-performance sample.
- **Freedom documentation fidelity:** the official Mac guide does acknowledge slower performance and desktop apps losing internet access, then recommends Non-Proxy Mode ([article dated 2026-03-11](https://support.freedom.to/en/articles/4385704-non-proxy-mode-for-mac)). Current official pages also contain the cited iOS 27 Connectivity Assist ([guide dated 2026-09-18](https://support.freedom.to/en/articles/4529937-freedom-for-ios-troubleshooting)) and Android 17 Advanced Protection limitations ([guide dated 2026-07-14](https://support.freedom.to/en/articles/4529927-freedom-for-android-troubleshooting)). These establish documented mechanisms/workarounds, not incidence.

### Inferences

- The notes’ best evidence is the combination of direct firsthand narrative, exact platform/version, and a dated vendor response. Changelog-only entries prove that a vendor published a fix claim, not how many users experienced or recovered from the defect.
- Aggregate Trustpilot pages and storefront listings are mutable and poor substitutes for review permalinks or archived snapshots. When no permalink exists, keep author/date/title with the quotation so the exact review can be re-identified.

### Gaps

- Indexed Reddit content and every individual review were not independently re-rendered; Reddit details not separately verified here should remain attributed and should not acquire stronger causal wording.
- No forensic logs, billing statements, support-ticket records, controlled benchmarks, or hands-on regression tests were available.

## Claims the final writer should avoid

### Takeaway

Avoid converting self-selected reports into prevalence, common root causes, permanent damage, abandonment, fraud, or systematic company wrongdoing. Also avoid absolute resolution claims where the vendor records are qualified or lack user confirmation.

### Cited Findings

- **All five products:** do not use “most users,” percentages, “typical resource use,” “systematically ignores support,” or “recurring” as a measured incidence rate. Independent anecdotes establish repeated themes only. The tiny [Focus review set](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/heyfocus.com) and mutable store/forum samples cannot support population estimates.
- **Cold Turkey:** do not state as fact that it destroyed years of work, modified BIOS, necessitated a factory reset, or creates unrecoverable locks. The strongest dramatic evidence remains unverified user narrative ([2026-03-26](https://uk.trustpilot.com/reviews/69c52539762069ec568a2c8c); [2026-07-07](https://uk.trustpilot.com/reviews/6ac27edf7df5762524253cc6)). Also do not treat third-party uninstall/SEO pages as technical corroboration; IObit and similar search hits were not primary or firsthand sources. Prefer [official recovery instructions](https://getcoldturkey.com/support/reinstall-instructions/) and the exact review permalink.
- **FocusMe:** do not call the 2023 expired-trial or 2025 Apple-Silicon experiences current permanent lockouts; removal occurred in the latter, and later changelog entries add remediation ([2025-12-09 vendor update](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/69326c42e2ab8b7ca471dcc3); [2026-10-04 changelog](https://focusme.com/download/changelog.txt)). Do not say current FocusMe generally loses unsaved Linux work: that explicit bug is tied to the fix in **8.1.4.6, 2026-10-30**.
- **Freedom:** do not repeat the corrected Android symptom list, claim version 7.14.1 was user-confirmed to fix it, or generalize one scheduled-session failure to all Android configurations ([2026-08-17/20 storefront exchange](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=to.freedom.android2&hl=en_US)). “Complete scam,” “no cancellation route,” “no refunds,” and “all browsing goes through Freedom’s cloud” remain unsupported/overbroad; use the [billing FAQ modified 2026-10-22](https://support.freedom.to/en/articles/4530040-freedom-subscription-and-billing-faq), [channel-specific deletion guidance](https://support.freedom.to/en/articles/11165071-how-to-delete-your-account), and [local-blocking privacy Q&A](https://support.freedom.to/en/articles/1347493-freedom-privacy-questions-and-answers) instead.
- **RescueTime:** do not claim systematic unauthorized subscription charging from the 2025–2026 anecdotes, a years-long continuous outage, or that the April 2026 incident definitely caused permanent user-data loss. Conversely, do not generalize November 2025’s “No records were lost” or the FAQ’s normal offline-sync assurance to every incident; the [April 24, 2026 official update](https://stspg.io/fdb8wg5vwzg0) expressly allowed a rare unrecoverable-file possibility. Use primary reviews and incident records rather than SEO summaries such as Neurolaunch.
- **SEO-only fresh results:** exclude ToolGPA’s Freedom refund inference and similar marketing/SEO summaries as corroboration. “Treat no stated policy as no refunds” is neither vendor policy nor firsthand evidence; use [Freedom’s actual terms](https://freedom.to/terms) or a relevant primary review instead.
- **heyfocus:** do not declare Focus abandoned or declare macOS Tahoe/26 incompatible. The [2026-06-19 forum post](https://talk.macpowerusers.com/t/focus-heyfocus-com-stop-supporting-and-abandon-development/45890), [2026-10-31 user post](https://johnbeales.com/micro/2026-10-31t114104-0400/), and [changelog whose latest listed release is 2025-08-01](https://heyfocus.com/changelog/) establish maintenance concerns and individual failures, not an abandonment announcement or a Tahoe compatibility test. Do not present the old 600-hour lock as Focus 2 behavior after the **2024-11-09** 24-hour cap.

### Inferences

- Use “a user reported,” “the vendor said,” “officially documented limitation,” and “later fix claimed” as distinct evidence classes. Do not collapse them into “confirmed,” especially for causal system-damage stories.
- Recurrence across different OSes, browser profiles, years, or releases is not evidence of one defect. Keep first-seen, last-confirmed, fixed-version, and user-confirmed-resolution fields separate.

### Gaps

- No independent legal/regulatory finding, representative complaint survey, security/privacy audit, or reproducible verification of the most destructive allegations was found.
- Vendor fix and recovery statements were not hand-tested. The final writer should not upgrade them to universally effective resolutions without affected-user confirmation or reproduction.
